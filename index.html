  • Tap Tap Reloaded

    A rhythm game for Android and iOS, made by a small team who cared a lot about the community around it. I did the backend, the Discord integration and the multiplayer server, which is written in Go.

    № 01 · Games + music · ● Open beta · Unity, Go, PHP, AWS

  • PartyZone

    A Minecraft server, mcpz.net (opens in new tab). It got a bit out of hand, and I sold it on so I could go and finish my degree.

    № 02 · Games · ○ Sold on · Java, PHP, Python, Redis

  • Swanzo

    Whenever I pay for something with my Monzo card, it checks me in at that venue on Swarm. Nobody asked for this. It has an admin panel.

    № 03 · Tools · ● Built · PHP (Symfony), Monzo and Swarm APIs

  • Alt1 apps

    Little overlays for RuneScape's Alt1 Toolkit. Each one reads something off the screen, does the sums and shows you the answer, so I can stop doing arithmetic in a video game. They'll tell you when an AFK task needs a click, what the next move is in the God Statues puzzle, or how many overload salves your bank can make.

    View project: Alt1 apps

    № 04 · Games + tools · ● Built · JavaScript, Alt1 Toolkit

  • MIDI to sheet music

    Turns MIDI files into sheet music, written in Haskell. It deliberately gives up some accuracy for readability, so you get something a person might actually be able to play.

    № 05 · Music · ● Finished · Haskell

  • Festival Playlist Generator

    Guesses what the bands will play at a festival and makes you a Spotify playlist of it, so you know the words before you get there. I built it for Download Festival. The catch is that Spotify keeps it in developer mode, so only accounts I've added can use it. You'll have to take my word for it.

    № 06 · Music + tools · ● Built · PHP (Symfony), Spotify API

  • Personal Life Blog

    Pulls data from Discord, Monzo, Swarm, Spotify, Trakt and Twitter and turns it into a timeline of what I've been up to. Self-surveillance, but as a blog.

    № 07 · Tools · ● Finished · Six APIs
