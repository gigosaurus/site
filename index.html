The bigger things, and the stories behind them.

Show

  • Tap Tap Reloaded

    A community-focused rhythm game for Android and iOS. I co-founded it and built the backend, the Discord integration and a multiplayer server in Go. The open beta was played by around 400,000 people.

    № 01 · Games + music · ● Open beta · Unity, Go, PHP, AWS

  • PartyZone

    Online multiplayer games that I started, ran with a remote team, and eventually sold on so I could finish my degree. More than 3 million people played them.

    № 02 · Games · ○ Sold on · Java, PHP, Python, Redis

  • Swanzo

    Checks me in to Swarm venues whenever my Monzo card is used there. Nobody had this problem, but now it's solved, with a customisable Symfony app and an admin panel.

    № 03 · Tools · ● Built · PHP (Symfony), Monzo and Swarm APIs

  • Alt1 apps

    Nine small overlays for RuneScape that watch the screen so I don't have to. They nag me when an AFK task needs a click, solve the God Statues puzzle and work out how many overload salves my bank can make.

    View project: Alt1 apps

    № 04 · Games + tools · ● Built · JavaScript, Alt1 Toolkit

  • MIDI to sheet music

    A Haskell command-line compiler that turns MIDI files into readable sheet music, knowingly trading precision for something a human can actually play. My final-year engineering project.

    № 05 · Music · ● Finished · Haskell

  • Festival Playlist Generator

    Predicts what the bands will play at a festival and builds you a playlist of it, so you can learn the songs before the crowd does. Made for Download Festival, with a timeline of set times. Pulls from Spotify, setlist.fm and MusicBrainz.

    View project: Festival Playlist Generator

    № 06 · Music + tools · ● Built · PHP (Symfony), React, Spotify API

  • Personal Life Blog

    A timeline of my own life, assembled automatically from Discord, Monzo, Swarm, Spotify, Trakt and Twitter. Part diary, part surveillance of myself.

    № 07 · Tools · ● Finished · Six APIs

ls Side quests

  • 26 Password Policy A password policy so absurd it looks impossible. It isn't, and you can work it out without guessing. There's a walkthrough for when you give up. ● Works
  • People picker Shuffles a list of people into pairs, keeping members of the same team apart. ● Works
  • Tap2Mid Drop in a Tap Tap track file, get a MIDI file out, with settings for approach speed and note offset. ● Works
  • Tap2Chart The same track files, turned into charts. ◐ Half-baked
  • Drop chance calculator Works out your odds of a rare RuneScape drop after a given number of kills. The news is usually bad. ● Works
  • Bluesky Quick Follow A small tool for following people on Bluesky in a hurry. ● Works
  • Battle Morale A game made at the Bristol Pride Game Jam 2018. I wrote a YAML-based script parser in Python so the non-programmers on the team could write the game themselves. ● Finished
